Gmail Job Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Gmail Job Assistant is a personal, open-source tool. Its only user is its author, who runs it on their own computer to sort their own job-search email. It reads mail and creates drafts; it never sends email. Your data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models by this app.

Who runs it

The app is written and operated by M Balasubramanya. The source code is public at github.com/balu0327/gmail-job-assistant. Anyone who installs it runs their own copy with their own Google sign-in; no data passes through the author.

What it accesses in Google

  • Read your email (gmail.readonly): sender, subject, date and text of recent messages, to decide which are job-search emails and what action they need.
  • Create drafts (gmail.compose): when you ask for a reply, it is saved to your Gmail Drafts folder. The app has no function that sends mail; you send drafts yourself.

It does not delete, archive, label or forward any email, and it does not access contacts, files or other Google data.

Where your data goes

  • Your computer. The app runs locally. It keeps a small local database of sorted emails (sender, subject, date, category, company, role and a one-line summary), run logs that contain timing and counts but no email text, and a status file for its local dashboard.
  • An AI model provider. To sort an email or draft a reply, the email's sender, subject and up to 4,000 characters of its text are sent to NVIDIA's hosted model API, or to a model running on the same computer (Ollama) if configured. NVIDIA's handling of those requests is governed by NVIDIA's own terms. Emails from known bulk job-alert senders are sorted by fixed rules and are not sent to any model.
  • Optional monitoring (off unless configured). If Langfuse monitoring is turned on, it receives timing, token counts, model names and categories. Email text is included only if that is explicitly enabled.

Nothing else receives your data. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described above: sorting job-search email, tracking applications and drafting replies for the user to review. It is not transferred to others except to the model provider as described above, which is needed to provide those features, and it is not used to train AI models by this app.

How long it is kept, and how to remove it

  • Local data stays until you delete it: remove tracker.db, logs/ and status/ from the app's folder.
  • To remove the app's access to your Gmail, go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove "Gmail Assistant", or delete token.json from the app's folder.
  • Drafts it created are ordinary Gmail drafts; delete them in Gmail as usual.

Contact
